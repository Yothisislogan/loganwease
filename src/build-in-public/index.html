What qualifies

A real process with a real user.

  • Insurance, sales, service, training, operations, technology, or community workflows
  • A clear person or team trying to complete a job
  • A visible workaround or repeated failure
  • Permission to discuss the process publicly or anonymously
  • A result that can be observed within the challenge
What does not

No hidden-data theater.

  • Requests requiring private customer or employee information
  • Projects where success cannot be defined
  • “Build my entire company for free” submissions
  • Work that depends on unauthorized system access
  • Problems that cannot be safely documented even anonymously
The public contract

No guaranteed happy ending.

The challenge guarantees a documented attempt, not a predetermined success story. A useful failure with clear evidence belongs in the Failure Files rather than being edited into a victory lap.

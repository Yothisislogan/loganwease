RIC Rail Connector Initiative

Williamsburg → Newport News → New Kent / Lanexa → Richmond International Airport

A phased public/private airport mobility corridor. The play: start with a reservation-based shuttle pilot timed to flight banks, use the real ridership data to prove demand, and only then ask whether a rail-adjacent airport connector deserves formal study. Shuttle-first, rail-ready.

The demand signal is already there — RIC set records in 2025, its own planning materials preserve a regional rail connection concept, and there is still no direct passenger rail station at the airport. The ask is modest: a 90-day discovery sprint with the right stakeholders, not a construction budget.

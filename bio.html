Logan Wease

Insurance founder, operator, and builder. Founder of We Insure Things.

Logan Wease is an insurance founder, operator, and builder focused on making complicated insurance and business systems easier to understand, sell, train, and operate. He is the founder of We Insure Things and works across agency growth, sales systems, training, customer experience, technology, and practical web workflows.

Before We Insure Things, Logan built Wease Financial, an Allstate agency in Williamsburg, Virginia — growing sales from $2.8M to $7.6M through three acquisitions, earning top-3% recognition among Allstate agencies nationwide, and selling the agency profitably in 2021. The hiring and training systems he developed there were adopted as best practice by agencies across the country, and he has keynoted a national forum of roughly 1,300 agents.

His work centers on building useful tools, stronger teams, and more human customer experiences in industries that often make simple things feel harder than they should.
